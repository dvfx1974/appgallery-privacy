Privacy Policy — Invoice Maker

Last updated: September 2026

Invoice Maker ("the app") is developed by Yousef Apps.

Data we collect

None. The app works fully offline. All data you enter (clients, invoices, business info) is stored only on your device in a local database. We never transmit, collect, or have access to any of your data.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. PDF sharing uses your device's standard share sheet — the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from your device.

Contact

For questions, contact the developer via the AppGallery listing page.
