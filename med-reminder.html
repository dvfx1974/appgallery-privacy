Privacy Policy — Med Reminder

Last updated: September 2026

Med Reminder ("the app") is developed by WolfDev.

Data we collect

None. The app works fully offline. All data you enter (medications, doses, family members) is stored only on your device in a local database. Your health data never leaves your phone.

Permissions

Notifications and alarms are used solely to remind you of your medication times. No data is transmitted with these reminders.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Medical disclaimer

The app is a reminder tool only and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from your device.

Contact

For questions, contact the developer WolfDev via the AppGallery listing page.
