Privacy Policy — Spy Camera Hunter

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Spy Camera Hunter ("the app") is developed by Yousef Ali Hussain Ali Alturki ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Data Collection

We collect nothing. Spy Camera Hunter works 100% offline. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no accounts.

2. Camera Permission

The camera is used only for the live lens-glint scan view. No photos or videos are ever captured, saved, or transmitted. The camera feed is shown on your screen in real time and discarded immediately.

3. Sensors

The magnetometer is read locally to display the magnetic field strength. Sensor data never leaves your device.

4. Local Storage

Your sweep-checklist progress and language preference are stored only on your device (local app storage). They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Internet Access

The app does not require an internet connection for any of its features.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us via the support email shown on our AppGallery listing.
